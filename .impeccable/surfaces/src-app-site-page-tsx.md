# Homepage surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Surface: `/` marketing homepage
- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: Fresno, Clovis, and Central Valley small-business owners who have a website but do not have an accountable partner improving it.
- Job: Make the cost of a neglected website obvious, make Hexacomb's ongoing ownership credible, remove risk objections, and drive a qualified contact-form submission.
- Primary action: Start a website growth conversation.

## Offer and proof

- Focus only on ongoing website management, aggressive local SEO, analytics interpretation, and conversion copy/marketing.
- Do not lead with custom software, AI automation, or general IT support.
- Proof comes from the real website-audit tool, the real founder/local presence, transparent plan terms, plain-English reporting, and specific recurring work—not invented clients, metrics, or testimonials.

## Chosen direction

- **Irrigation Canal Blueprint** (seed `5fa491f1`, assigned candidate 3; degraded roll, no challengers).
- Thesis: Continuous website ownership is Central Valley irrigation — care must keep flowing through the search → site → call gates or the field goes dry.
- World: sun-bleached concrete, canal teal water fields, blueprint navy ink, sluice-gate citrus for conversion.
- Memorable moments: brand-first HEXACOMB title block; sticky scrub of the call path; hold-to-open sluice gate; numbered workstream ledger; hotspot report + founder gallery beat.
- Craft references: Seasats sticky chapters, Kalkbrenner brand presence, Zero-style gate spice — not WebGL or audio.

## Constraints

- Bright-light readable, WCAG AA, responsive, motion-reduced fallback.
- No generic icon-card grid, glass nav, honeycomb/bee motifs, fake social proof, fake analytics, or broad technology-service claims.
- Preserve real routes, tracking attributes, Turnstile contact behavior, SEO metadata, and structured data.
