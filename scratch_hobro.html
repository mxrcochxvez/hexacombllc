BRINC Drones

BRINC is a mission-driven public safety drone company building life-saving tech for first responders. We teamed up early on, helping them rethink their digital presence from the ground up.

We started with a full website redesign and grew into a deeper partnership—social graphics, event materials, investor decks, printed collateral, and more. As their digital partner, we’ve helped shape a brand that scales with ambition. In 2025, BRINC raised $75M and announced a strategic partnership with Motorola Solutions—milestones we were proud to support.

Bridge Health

When BridgeHealth came to Hobro, they had a clear product idea and a partially built backend. They needed a partner to help design the MVP, build the front-end, and integrate the APIs developed by their own engineering team. Working side by side with BridgeHealth’s backend developers, we turned the early concept into a cohesive, functional platform.

Since then, we’ve continued to support BridgeHealth as an extension of their team, contributing to ongoing product improvements and interface evolution. We also designed and developed their marketing website and landing pages, complete with product animations built in Lottie to illustrate how the technology works in action.

BRINC LiveOps

When BRINC reached out to Hobro, they weren’t looking for surface-level support — they needed a partner to help build a mission-critical platform from the ground up. LiveOps had to unify live video, two-way comms, fleet management, 3D mapping, and evidence handling into a seamless, reliable interface for real-time emergencies.

We led the full product design cycle, built the entire front end, and contributed deeply to the backend alongside BRINC’s engineering team. Today, LiveOps powers 700+ public safety agencies across the U.S.

DV Commodities

DV Commodities is a division within DV Group, a global proprietary trading firm. Formerly operating as DV Energy since 2013, the division rebranded to DV Commodities in 2025 to reflect its expansion into metals, softs, and agricultural commodities, alongside its established energy trading operations.

We partnered with the team during this transition—developing a digital presence from scratch, including foundational branding guidelines, a new website, and launch support. We also assisted with corporate branding across social and internal channels, helping position DV Commodities for its next chapter.

Your Best Credit Cards

Your Best Credit Cards is an affiliate site helping people find the best credit cards based on how they actually spend. When they came to us, the product was outdated—confusing UX, rigid structure, and low conversion. None of it matched the ambition behind the idea.

We fully rebranded and rebuilt the platform from the ground up. That meant UX research, content strategy, and a highly customized backend that calculates rewards based on detailed spending profiles. We also designed and developed a logged-in user experience. Since launch, the client has seen a clear lift in conversions.

Core Vision

At Hobro Digital, our passion lies in crafting fresh digital presences that help our clients stand out in today's dynamic digital landscape. Designing compelling brand narratives and innovative digital experiences is not just what we do — it's what we love.
We think of our team like a creative community, almost like a small town where every client is welcome, feels valued and supported. We love our people, every day we ensure that our team has a great working environment and space for creativity and personal development.
