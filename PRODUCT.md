# Hexacomb — Product context

## Register

**Brand** — marketing and landing surfaces where trust and clarity are the product.

## Users

Small business owners in Fresno, Clovis, and the Central Valley. Often non-technical. Checking the site on a phone between jobs, in bright daylight. They need to know quickly: Is this person real, local, and going to actually handle things?

## Product purpose

Position Hexacomb as the single technology partner who takes websites, software, AI workflows, and day-to-day IT off the owner's plate. No jargon, no vendor juggling.

## Brand personality

- **Plain** — says what it does in words a shop owner would use
- **Local** — Fresno/Clovis is identity, not a footnote
- **Dependable** — calm, competent, answers the phone

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing (orange gradient hero, icon card grid, dark contact band)
- AI slop (DM Sans, uppercase section kickers, glass nav, hero metrics)
- Developer aesthetic (terminal green, monospace decoration, dark mode by default)
- Agency fluff ("boutique", "synergy", animated bees/honey branding)

## Strategic design principles

1. Light surfaces — owners read this outdoors; contrast must be obvious.
2. One accent color, used sparingly; warmth comes from tinted neutrals, not orange floods.
3. Photography of real work contexts beats abstract tech illustration.
4. Layout is editorial-left, not centered marketing stack.
5. Services read as a clear list, not four identical cards.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast on body text. Focus states visible. Forms labeled. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
